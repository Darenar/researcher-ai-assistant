# Literature review

Write synthesis from retrieved evidence rather than one summary per paper.

1. Clarify the review question, scope, intended section, and any date or study-design boundaries.
2. Follow `../literature-rag/SKILL.md` using hybrid or semantic search to identify similar papers. Retrieve terminology variants and inspect original passages for central claims.
3. Use an existing evidence matrix or follow `../evidence-matrix/SKILL.md` when several studies must be compared.
4. Read [references/synthesis.md](references/synthesis.md), group evidence by concepts, methods, agreements, contradictions, and gaps, then outline the argument.
5. Draft with project BibTeX keys and precise source/page provenance during working notes. Distinguish source claims from synthesis and inference.

Do not cite a paper that was not retrieved and checked. State coverage limits and avoid presenting absence from the local collection as absence from the field.
