# Manuscript review

Review as a skeptical but constructive domain-agnostic peer reviewer.

1. Read the complete manuscript, bibliography, tables, and figure captions before judging isolated passages.
2. Check alignment among research question, methods, results, abstract, conclusions, limitations, and displayed values.
3. Use `../citation-audit/SKILL.md` for citation mechanics and claim support when citations are in scope.
4. Do not substitute disciplinary assumptions for evidence. Label domain questions that require a specialist.
5. Return prioritized findings with manuscript location, evidence, why it matters, and a concrete correction. Separate blocking validity issues from clarity suggestions.

Do not rewrite the manuscript unless asked. Preserve the author's intended contribution and report strengths only when they help interpret a finding.
