# Systematic review

Maintain an auditable review record; do not replace reviewer judgment.

1. Read [references/review-records.md](references/review-records.md) and establish the question framework, eligibility criteria, sources, dates, and complete search strategies before screening.
2. Assign stable record IDs. Deduplicate without deleting the original import record.
3. Record each title/abstract and full-text decision with reviewer, timestamp, stage, and a controlled exclusion reason. Never fabricate a second reviewer or consensus.
4. Use `../evidence-matrix/SKILL.md` for included-study extraction and keep risk-of-bias assessments distinct from general notes.
5. Compute flow counts from the records rather than typing totals manually. Flag inconsistent transitions or missing reasons.

Treat automation as assistance: uncertain eligibility remains `unclear` for human resolution.
